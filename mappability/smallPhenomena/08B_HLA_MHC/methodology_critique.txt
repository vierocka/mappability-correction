Methodological critique — Gong & Karchin (2023)
================================================
"Clustering by antigen-presenting genes reveals immune landscapes and predicts
response to checkpoint immunotherapy."  Sci. Reports 13:950.

Core question
-------------
Does the prognostic signal arise from specific HLA/APM genes, or from broader
transcriptional activity of the highly duplicated and polymorphic HLA region?

Short answer: the evidence points to a locus-level regional co-expression signal,
not gene-specific protein biology.  Four lines of argument follow.


1. The pseudogene inclusion is the smoking gun
-----------------------------------------------
HLA-DRB6 (a transcribed_unprocessed_pseudogene, no protein product, GENCODE v49)
was forward-selected into the 23-gene prognostic APM signature alongside functional
genes.

If the signal were gene-specific — each protein contributing independently to
antigen presentation — a pseudogene with no protein product could not carry
prognostic value on its own terms.  The only explanations are:

  a) Its "expression" is read leakage from DRB1/3/4/5 (mappability artifact),
     making it a noisy proxy for functional DRB expression; or
  b) It is a proxy for the entire HLA region being transcriptionally active.

Either interpretation means the selection process captured a regional co-expression
signal, not HLA-DRB6 protein biology.

2. Forward selection on a correlated, non-uniquely mappable gene set cannot isolate
   gene-specific contributions
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All HLA class I genes are co-regulated by the same IFN-γ / NF-κB axis.  In bulk
RNA-seq their TPM values are structurally correlated because:

  Biology:      All class I genes respond to the same inflammatory stimuli.
                A sample with high HLA-A will have high HLA-B, HLA-C, HLA-E.

  Mappability:  At 100 bp reads, 60–80% of k-mers are shared between class I
                paralogs and their pseudogenes (HLApseudo_MAFFT shared_fraction.tsv).
                EM-based redistributors (kallisto/Salmon) assign ambiguous reads
                proportionally to current abundance estimates, coupling all estimates
                within the same locus.

Forward selection on this correlated matrix picks up the strongest predictor of the
composite locus signal, not the causal gene.  Swapping HLA-B for HLA-A, or removing
any single gene and replacing it with another co-regulated HLA gene, would likely
retain the same C-index.  The 23-gene composition is an artefact of the specific
cohort and the particular correlated structure of the training data.


3. EM redistribution in tumours with pseudogene derepression manufactures
   cross-sample correlation
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
In melanoma, global hypomethylation and chromatin remodelling at 6p21 derepress
HLA pseudogene promoters in a tumour-specific, sample-variable manner.  Samples
with higher pseudogene transcription have a larger ambiguous read pool.

kallisto/Salmon redistribute more reads across all DRB and class I genes in those
samples.  This creates an artificial positive correlation between HLA gene estimates
and the tumour immune landscape that is not mediated by protein surface expression —
it tracks pseudogene activity instead.  This is not a constant bias that cancels in
differential expression: it is condition-differential and enriched precisely in the
cancer samples driving the prognostic association.

See: HLApseudo_MAFFT/README.md (section on "Pseudogene transcription" and
     "Consequence for RNA-seq")
     HLApseudo_MAFFT/notes.txt (3'UTR rescue — the only clean signal)


4. The paper's "normalisation" does not address mappability
------------------------------------------------------------
The authors applied a correction factor based on tumour purity and per-cell HLA
expression levels to adjust for immune cell contamination in bulk RNA-seq.  This
corrects for cell-type composition confound (HLA class II expression mirrors
immune infiltration, not tumour HLA status) but does NOT address:

  - Read ambiguity within the HLA locus
  - Pseudogene derepression inflating the ambiguous pool
  - Aligner-dependent redistribution differences between samples

After purity correction, the residual signal still contains locus-level co-expression
plus mappability coupling between paralogs and pseudogenes.

Conclusion
----------
The Gong & Karchin APM signature is likely measuring "HLA locus transcriptional
activity level" — a real biological quantity (IFN-γ responsiveness, immune
infiltration) — but the 23-gene decomposition is not justified by the data.  The
specific gene contributions cannot be separated from locus-level co-expression and
mappability coupling.  The inclusion of HLA-DRB6 (pseudogene) provides the clearest
single piece of evidence that the selection process was not sensitive to protein-level
biology.

Independent validation should require at minimum:
  - IHC or flow cytometry for HLA surface expression (not RNA-seq derived)
  - WES/WGS for LOH at 6p21 (to distinguish silencing from allele loss)
  - Long-read RNA-seq to resolve transcripts across inactivating mutations
  - Personalised HLA-typed reference to reduce allelic ambiguity before quantification
