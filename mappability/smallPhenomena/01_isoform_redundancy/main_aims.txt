- check UTR, CDS, protein coding domains
- at least 2 unique 31-mers in a contiguous stretch spanning ≥ 50 bp total
  (optimal: ≥ 20 consecutive unique 31-mer positions = 50 bp span;
   minimum reportable: 1 unique 31-mer = 31 bp span)
- f_uniq > 0.95 (uniqueness factor)
- try per gene
- try per isoform

2.) define set of genes where using isoform level is pointless

3.) define genes and isoforms with uniqueness level 0 (at raw CDS, at UTR-CDS-UTR level)

4.) define genes with zero or hugely inflated signal (RNA -> immunopeptidomics difficulties; does applying f_uniq always help?)

5.) evolutionary perspective - isoforms, pseudogenes, single genes, multicopy genes, their homology, conservation and uniqueness

FEASIBILITY AND PRIOR KNOWLEDGE ASSESSMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
k-mer note: k = 31 throughout (KMER_K constant in analyze.py; matches kallisto/salmon
default). Rather than raising k, uniqueness is assessed via contiguous stretches of
unique 31-mers: a run of N consecutive positions where every 31-mer is unique spans
N + 30 bp. Target stretch: ≥ 50 bp (≥ 20 consecutive unique positions). This tolerates
short indels or mismatches that shift k-mer positions by a few bp while guaranteeing
≥ 1 unique 31-mer within any overlapping read. One unique 31-mer (31 bp) is the minimum
reportable case. Key output columns: max_unique_stretch_bp, stretch_reaches_50bp.

Aim 1 — Targeted sequence list for scRNA (unique k-mer approach)
   Feasible  : Yes. All inputs exist. The global k-mer frequency table is built in
               Section C (parse_mane_fasta). The unique_kmers column in
               isoform_kmers_mane.tsv stores the count of unique 50-mers per
               transcript; filtering on unique_kmers ≥ 2 AND frac_unique ≥ 0.95
               gives the target gene set directly. Per-isoform variant uses
               GENCODE pc_transcripts.fa (already parsed in Section G). Domain-region
               windowing requires the planned hmmscan step (to_does.txt, DOMAIN-LEVEL
               MAPPABILITY section).
   Known     : The splici index (Tian et al. 2023, alevin-fry) identifies unique
               k-mers per gene for scRNA quantification — the closest published analog.
               Probe design for targeted RNA capture uses equivalent logic. A MANE-
               anchored table with per-gene minimum unique k-mer counts and domain-
               region annotation at k=50 is not published.

Aim 2 — Gene list where isoform-level quantification is pointless
   Feasible  : Yes. Section F (utr_fail_stats.tsv, signal_distribution column) and
               Section H (gene_level_recommendation.tsv) produce this list directly.
               Inputs in place: isoform_kmers_mane.tsv + kallisto est_counts.
   Known     : The general limitation of short-read isoform quantification is
               documented (Glaus et al. 2012, Turro et al. 2011, Patro et al. 2017).
               SUPPA2, rMATS, and DEXSeq implicitly restrict to separable splicing
               events. A curated genome-wide list of MANE genes where isoform-level
               TPM is empirically uninformative (k-mer + entropy + chi-squared) is
               not published.

Aim 3 — Genes with frac_unique = 0 at CDS and at UTR-CDS-UTR level
   Feasible  : Yes. Filter isoform_kmers_cds.tsv and isoform_kmers_mane.tsv on
               frac_unique == 0 and unique_kmers == 0. Already computed; needs
               tabulation.
   Known     : Y-chromosome multicopy genes (BPY2, DAZ, VCY, TSPY) and certain
               tandem arrays (PRR20, AMY1) are qualitatively known to be unmappable
               (genome assembly and population genetics literature). A MANE-anchored
               catalog with exact frac_unique = 0 at transcript level at k=50 is
               not published.

Aim 4 — Inflated signal; does f_uniq correction always help?
   Feasible  : Yes for RNA side. Comparing vs_dedup_cds vs vs_MANE kallisto entropy
               (Section D) identifies genes where applying the MANE index provides no
               improvement — the cases where f_uniq does not help. The immunopeptidomics
               link requires experimental peptide tables (not in the current pipeline).
   Known     : Pseudogene-derived peptides in immunopeptidomics are documented in
               individual cases (HLA pseudogenes: Faridi et al. 2018). A systematic
               genome-wide analysis linking RNA-level mappability uniqueness to
               immunopeptidomics signal inflation is not published. The f_uniq
               correction approach is novel.

Aim 5 — Evolutionary perspective (isoforms, pseudogenes, multicopy genes,
         homology, conservation, uniqueness)
   Feasible  : Partially. Pseudogene counts (Section B), isoform homology (Section E),
               paralog pairwise identity (Section G) are implemented. Conservation
               is not covered — would require dN/dS ratios (Ensembl Compara) or
               per-base conservation scores (phastCons/phyloP, UCSC) mapped onto
               transcript coordinates. Evolutionary age of paralogs (Ks values or
               synteny-based dating) is not in the current pipeline.
   Known     : The relationship between gene family size, pseudogene accumulation,
               and mappability difficulty is understood qualitatively (Zhang 2003 on
               gene duplication; OR gene literature; HLA immunogenetics). A
               quantitative framework connecting isoform count × pseudogene count ×
               pairwise identity × k-mer uniqueness × UF in a single analysis is
               not published.
